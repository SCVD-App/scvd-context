# Pro Access Recovery — Support Form Letter

Works for any Ancient Games app (Hnefatafl, Jumpin' Pin, Two Ancient Classics) — all three
now share the same restore flow. Swap the bracketed bits, everything else stays as-is.

---

## Copy-paste template

> Hey [NAME],
>
> Sorry for the hassle — this is a quick fix and you won't lose your purchase. Cache clears
> and new devices both trigger this; it happens.
>
> **To get Pro back on this device:**
>
> 1. Open [GAME NAME]: [APP URL]
> 2. Tap **"Restore Purchase"**
> 3. Enter the email you used when you originally paid
> 4. Check that inbox — you'll get an email with an **"Unlock Pro on this device"** button.
>    Tap it, and you're back in immediately.
>
> **If the button doesn't open right** (happens sometimes depending on your email app), the
> same email has a backup code near the bottom. Instead of tapping the button:
>
> 1. Back in [GAME NAME], tap **"have a code already?"**
> 2. Paste that code in and hit **Apply**
>
> Either way gets you to the same place — the button's just faster.
>
> **If no email arrives within a few minutes:**
> - Check spam/junk first
> - Make sure it's the *exact* email you paid with — a different email won't find your purchase
> - You can only request one restore email every 5 minutes, so if you asked twice quickly, wait
>   a moment and it'll come through
>
> Still stuck? Reply here and I'll sort it out directly.
>
> — [YOUR NAME]

## Notes for reuse (not part of the message — delete before sending)

- **Button/link labels are now identical across all three apps** — "Restore Purchase" and
  "have a code already?" — so this template doesn't need per-app wording changes, just the
  game name and URL.
- **The 5-minute rate limit is real and shared logic**, not a guess — worth keeping that line
  in, since "why didn't my email arrive" is the single most likely follow-up question.
- **Two Ancient Classics** covers both Ur and Nine Men's Morris with one purchase — if someone
  writes in confused about which game to open Restore from, either works, since Pro status is
  shared across the whole bundle.
- If someone's hit the **10-device activation cap** (very rare — that's a lot of devices), the
  app already tells them this directly rather than a generic failure. Worth just pointing them
  to support@scvd.app at that point rather than troubleshooting further yourself.
