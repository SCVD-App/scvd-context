Camps Australia Deal Structure
What are they worth to us?
Their 15,500+ verified sites, 30 years of ground-truthing, and community trust are the data moat that would take us years to replicate. Here are three deal structures — with a recommendation.
Option A — Annual Flat Licence
FAIR
We pay Camps Australia a fixed annual fee for the right to use their data in the app. Simple, predictable for both sides. Good if our revenue underperforms — they still get paid.
Year 1: $5,000–$8,000/yr
Year 2+: $10,000–$15,000/yr
Renegotiate at 10k users
Option B — Revenue Share (Recommended)
RECOMMENDED
Camps Australia receives a percentage of net revenue from every paid subscriber who uses the Long Haul / Camps AU data feature. Aligns incentives perfectly — they win when we win. Fairer than flat fee if we scale fast.
10–15% of net revenue
Minimum guarantee: $3k/yr
Quarterly reporting + audit rights
Option C — Hybrid (Flat + Share)
ALSO FAIR
A lower flat annual fee providing security for Camps Australia, plus a smaller revenue share kicking in once we hit $50k annual revenue. Reduces their risk in the early stages while maintaining upside.
$3,000/yr flat base
+ 8% rev share after $50k
Grows naturally with scale

Their existing book: Camps Australia's annual publication retails around $50–60. If 200,000 app users buy one book, that's $10M+ in book revenue — we add to that, not compete with it.
The "Camps AU Verified" badge: Every facility we show from their data carries their brand. Every time a user taps that badge, it's an advertisement for the Camps Australia publication. We're their marketing channel.
Be honest about timing: Year 1 revenue will be small. The value to Camps Australia in Year 1 is brand exposure and market positioning, not just cash. Make sure the letter acknowledges that.
What to Walk In With
Based on conservative projections, Camps Australia can expect meaningful income from Year 2 onwards, growing significantly by Year 3. The revenue share model is the right structure — it removes risk for both sides and aligns incentives perfectly. They only earn more when GottaGo grows, which means they have skin in the game and a reason to support the partnership actively.

The non-cash value — brand exposure to a new digital audience, the "Camps AU Verified" badge on every relevant facility, and positioning as the trusted data partner of Australia's most innovative navigation app — is worth more in the long run than any Year 1 cheque.
